            Calculation methods

            Each bar shows how far that month's price usually runs above or below the average of the 12 months around it, averaged over the years shown. Measuring against the months around it keeps a rising market from making the late months look better than they were. Changes in the grade mix are taken out too. Thin lines on each bar show the lowest and highest year. A heavy weight group with too few sales for a pattern uses the nearest lighter group's pattern.

            About these numbers

            Prices come from USDA Agricultural Marketing Service auction reports.

            How these numbers are calculated
            • Feeder prices are Medium and Large frame calves graded #1, #1-2 and #2, in dollars per hundredweight (cwt). Only regular lots count: Value Added, Fancy, Fleshy and other described lots, and #3 calves, are left out. Cull cows and slaughter bulls are Average dressing. Prices are averaged by head; USDA's report averages are weighted by pounds, so the two can differ by a few cents.
            • The statewide view combines every reporting auction in the state. Graded, special and board sales are not included.
            • Market reporters grade and price part of each sale (often 40-90%), and prices here come only from the cattle they priced; the About box on This week gives that share. Likely reporting errors are left out, such as a price more than three times, or less than a fifth of, the national typical price for similar cattle that month.
